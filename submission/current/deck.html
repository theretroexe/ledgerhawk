Control and auditability

Failures and overrides stay visible.

Deterministic result

Duplicate invoices are rejected. Other high-risk issues escalate. Remaining mandatory failures hold. Clean invoices approve. A conflicting assessment is overridden and logged.

Human review

Exceptions enter a review queue. A release of an escalated invoice requires a named reviewer, reason, and call-back attestation.

Live failure

A failed or timed-out Evolus call is shown as an error. The app does not silently simulate a successful run or append an audit entry.

Prototype boundary

In-memory ledger and local master data. No authentication, outbound email, ERP posting, or payment execution.

The Audit tab's seeded figures are illustrative calculations, not measured customer results.
