LedgerHawk

An accounts-payable agent that reads every supplier invoice, checks it against the PO and vendor master, and stamps it before any money moves.

Evolus extractor · agent · knowledge library · AI Tool · workflows Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct on AMD Instinct MI300X · vLLM

The problem

AP teams key invoices by hand, and the expensive mistakes still get paid.

Manual keying
10–15 min

per invoice to read it, type it into the ERP, match it to the PO and chase the vendor. Most of that time goes on clean invoices that need no judgement.

Duplicate payments
"Second notice"

A reminder for an invoice that was already paid looks like a new invoice. Pay it again and the money is gone until someone notices.

Bank-detail fraud
"Our IBAN changed"

Business email compromise: a real-looking invoice with new bank details. One of the costliest frauds companies face, and it lands in the AP inbox.

The 10–15 minute figure is a working assumption for manual handling; the value slide uses 12 minutes.

LedgerHawk

Who it is for

Two people own every supplier payment. LedgerHawk does the routine part for both.

AP clerk

Works the inbox, keys invoices, chases vendors.

  • Clean invoices are approved for payment without a touch; the clerk only sees exceptions.
  • Price or quantity variances arrive with the vendor email already drafted.
  • The evidence is written in the margin next to the exact field that failed.

Financial controller

Owns payment risk, signs off on exceptions, answers the auditors.

  • Duplicates and bank-detail changes are stopped before payment, not found after.
  • Releasing a stopped invoice takes a name, a reason and a call-back attestation.
  • An audit log with CSV export records every decision and every override.

Most invoices never reach a person, and the risky ones never reach the bank.

At scale (illustrative)

2,000 invoices/month × 70% × 12 min ≈ 280 hours a month, about 1.75 AP clerks.

Assumptions: 30-day synthetic demo ledger plus the 5 demo emails, not customer data. Loss prevented counts only duplicates and suspected fraud. 12 min is the midpoint of the 10–15 min manual estimate.

Guardrail

The model can be stricter than the rules, never looser.

  • Rules set the floor. Eight controls run in code, so an auditor can replay them.
  • The model adds judgement. It may stop a clean-looking invoice; it cannot approve a flagged one.
  • Releasing a Stop is a control. Reviewer name, reason and a call-back on the number in the vendor master.
  • No silent failures. A failed live call falls back visibly, with the error shown.
if d == "approve" and flags:
